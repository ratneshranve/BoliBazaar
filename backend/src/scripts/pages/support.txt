ACCOUNT AND LOGIN

I am not getting the OTP.
Check that the number is correct and that your phone has network signal. Wait 60 seconds and tap Resend OTP. If you use a dual-SIM phone, make sure SMS is enabled for that SIM. If it still does not arrive, contact us with your mobile number.

I changed my mobile number.
Write to us from your registered email address, or open a request from the app, and we will help you move your account to the new number after checking it is yours.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile › My data & account › Delete my account. Your account is deleted after 15 days. If you sign in again during those 15 days, the deletion is cancelled.

POSTING AND MANAGING ADS

How do I post an ad?
Tap Sell, choose a category, add clear photos, a title, description, price and location, and tap Post. Some ads are checked by our team before they go live.

Why was my ad rejected or removed?
Ads are removed when they break our Terms or Prohibited Items Policy, for example wrong category, misleading details, banned items, duplicate ads, or phone numbers and links inside the ad. The reason is shown in My ads. Edit the ad and submit it again, or contact us if you think it was a mistake.

How do I mark my item as sold?
Open My ads, tap the ad and choose Mark as sold. This stops new buyers from contacting you.

How do promotions work?
Featured, Top and Urgent promotions show your ad more prominently for the period you choose. They increase visibility but do not guarantee a sale.

AUCTIONS (BOLI)

How does bidding work?
Open an auction, enter your bid (at least the next bid step) or set a maximum amount and the app will bid for you only as much as needed. If someone bids in the last minutes, the auction may be extended so everyone gets a fair chance.

I won an auction. What next?
Open Profile › My Purchases. Contact the seller, inspect the item and complete the deal within the time shown. If you do not complete a won deal, you may receive a strike.

Can I cancel a bid?
Bids are binding offers to buy. If you placed a bid by mistake, contact us immediately with the auction details.

PAYMENTS AND REFUNDS

I was charged but my plan or promotion is not active.
It can take a few minutes for the payment to confirm. If it is still not active after 30 minutes, contact us with the payment reference shown in Payments. Double charges are always refunded.

How long does a refund take?
Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days.

Where is my invoice?
Open Payments in your profile and tap any payment to view or download its invoice.

STAY SAFE

• Meet in a public place and inspect the item before you pay.
• Never pay in advance to someone you have not met.
• Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card details or passwords with anyone, including people who say they are from Boli Bazaar. We will never ask for them.
• You never need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
• Check documents such as RC, ownership papers and bills before buying vehicles, property or costly items.
• If something feels wrong, stop and report the user from the ad or chat.

REPORT A PROBLEM

To report a fake ad, a scam or abusive behaviour, tap Report on the ad, profile or chat. For fraud complaints, open Help Centre › Report a fraud. If you have lost money, also report it on the national cyber crime helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.
